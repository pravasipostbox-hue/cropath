Emergency Help in Croatia

Important emergency numbers and practical information for people living, working or travelling in Croatia.

Important Emergency Numbers

In an emergency, stay calm and contact the appropriate emergency service. If you are unsure which service you need, call 112.

🚨
112

General Emergency

European emergency number for situations requiring urgent assistance from emergency services.

📞 Call 112
🚑
194

Emergency Medical Service

Use this number when urgent medical assistance or an ambulance is required.

📞 Call 194
👮
192

Police

Contact the police for urgent police assistance, serious incidents or immediate danger.

📞 Call 192
🔥
193

Fire Brigade

Contact the fire brigade for fires and situations requiring urgent firefighting assistance.

📞 Call 193
⚠️ If You Are In Immediate Danger

Move to a safe place if possible and call 112. Clearly explain where you are and what has happened. Follow the instructions given by the emergency operator.

What Information Should You Give?

1. Your Location
Give the exact address or the nearest recognizable location.
2. What Happened?
Briefly explain the emergency and what assistance is needed.
3. How Many People Need Help?
Tell the operator approximately how many people are involved.
4. Follow Instructions
Stay on the line and follow the operator's instructions.

Medical Emergency

For a serious medical emergency, call 194 or 112.

  • Keep the person safe while waiting for help.
  • Do not move an injured person unless necessary for safety.
  • Have identification and health information available if possible.
  • Follow instructions from emergency medical personnel.

For Foreigners in Croatia

If you do not speak Croatian well, tell the emergency operator that you need language assistance. Give your location clearly and use simple words if necessary.

Important

CROPATH is an information guide and is not an emergency service. In an actual emergency, contact the appropriate Croatian emergency service directly. Do not wait for a response from CROPATH.
