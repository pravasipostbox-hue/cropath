Family in Croatia

Useful information about bringing family members, children, school, healthcare and everyday family life in Croatia.

1. Family members in Croatia

Foreign workers and residents in Croatia may have options for family members to join them, depending on their residence status and applicable Croatian rules.

  • Spouse or partner, where applicable
  • Children
  • Other family members in situations permitted by law
  • Required documents depend on the type of residence application
Residence rules can differ depending on the applicant's situation. Always check the latest requirements with the Croatian authorities.

2. Family reunification

Family reunification may allow eligible family members of a foreign resident to apply for residence in Croatia.

The procedure can involve identity documents, proof of family relationship, accommodation, health insurance and other required documents.

3. Important documents

  • Valid passport or identity document
  • Birth certificate, where required
  • Marriage certificate, where applicable
  • Proof of family relationship
  • Proof of accommodation, when required
  • Health insurance or other required evidence
  • Other documents requested by the authorities
Foreign documents may need translation, certification or legalisation depending on the document and procedure.

4. Children

Families moving to Croatia should arrange residence documentation and other required registrations for children.

  • Keep children's passports and documents valid.
  • Check residence requirements.
  • Arrange health insurance when required.
  • Keep birth certificates and family documents safely.
  • Check school or kindergarten options in your area.

5. School and education

Children living in Croatia may attend education according to Croatian education rules and their individual circumstances.

  • Kindergarten and preschool options
  • Primary school
  • Secondary school
  • Language support for children who do not speak Croatian
  • Recognition of previous education, when applicable
gov.hr

6. Healthcare for family

Families should understand their healthcare and health insurance arrangements in Croatia.

  • Family doctor / primary healthcare
  • Paediatric care for children
  • Hospitals and specialist care
  • Pharmacies
  • Emergency medical services
HZZO

7. Address and registration

Family members living in Croatia may need to register their address or residence according to their legal status.

  • Keep proof of accommodation.
  • Check the correct registration procedure.
  • Keep copies of submitted documents.
  • Report changes when required.

8. Family documents

Keep important family documents together and safely stored.

  • Passports
  • Residence permits
  • Birth certificates
  • Marriage certificate
  • School documents
  • Health insurance documents
  • Important Croatian administrative documents

9. If your family moves to Croatia

Before moving, prepare documents and check the requirements that apply to each family member.

  • Check passports and document validity.
  • Check residence requirements.
  • Arrange accommodation.
  • Check health insurance.
  • Research schools or childcare.
  • Keep translated documents ready when required.
  • Save important emergency contacts.

10. Official information

For current information about residence, family reunification and public services, use official Croatian sources.

MUP Croatia gov.hr HZZO

11. Important

Residence, family reunification, school and healthcare requirements can change. Always confirm the latest information with the relevant Croatian authority.
