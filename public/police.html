Important information about police registration, certificates, addresses and common documents for foreigners in Croatia.

✓ Information checked: 7 October 2026

Police in Croatia

The Croatian police, under the Ministry of the Interior (MUP), handle many matters related to foreigners, residence permits, registrations and identity documents.

Depending on your situation, you may need to contact the competent police administration or police station.

Important: Always check which police administration or police station is responsible for your place of residence before visiting.

Common Documents & Services

  • Residence registration and residence-related procedures
  • Residence permits and cards
  • Address registration and changes
  • Certificates and official records
  • Foreigner identification documents
  • Reporting changes in personal circumstances
  • Other procedures handled by MUP

Address Registration

Foreigners staying in Croatia may have obligations concerning registration of their address or place of stay.

Requirements can depend on the type of stay, residence status, accommodation and individual circumstances.

Do not guess the procedure. If you are unsure whether you need to register, change or report an address, check with the competent MUP office.

Police Certificates

Some employers, authorities and immigration procedures may require official certificates or records.

The exact document depends on the purpose. Before requesting a certificate, confirm the exact name of the document required by the employer, authority or institution.

Tip: If a document is required for use outside Croatia, also check whether translation, certification, apostille or another form of authentication is required.

When Visiting MUP

Take important documents

  • Passport or valid travel document
  • Residence card or permit, if applicable
  • Relevant application or appointment documents
  • Supporting documents required for your procedure

Before You Go

  • Check the correct MUP office.
  • Check whether an appointment is required.
  • Check current document requirements.
  • Check opening hours before travelling.
