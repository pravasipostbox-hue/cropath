Banking in Croatia

Understand bank accounts, cards, payments, ATMs and basic banking services in Croatia.

1. Opening a bank account

Foreigners living or working in Croatia may be able to open a bank account, depending on their residence status and the bank's requirements.

  • Passport or valid identity document
  • Residence permit or other proof of legal stay, when required
  • OIB (Croatian Personal Identification Number)
  • Proof of address may be requested
  • Additional documents may be requested by the bank

2. OIB

OIB is the Croatian Personal Identification Number. It is commonly used for banking, employment, tax and many other official procedures.

Keep your OIB information safely because you may need it for many services in Croatia.

3. Bank card

After opening an account, the bank may provide a debit card. Your bank will explain how to activate and use the card.

  • ATM withdrawals
  • Card payments in shops
  • Online payments
  • Contactless payments
  • Mobile banking, if available

4. ATM use

You can use ATMs to withdraw cash and perform other services supported by your bank.

  • Check the withdrawal amount before confirming.
  • Read any fee information shown on the screen.
  • Keep your PIN private.
  • Do not give your card or PIN to another person.
  • Contact your bank immediately if your card is lost or stolen.

5. Mobile and internet banking

Croatian banks commonly provide mobile or internet banking services. Availability and features depend on the bank.

  • Check account balance
  • View transactions
  • Make payments
  • Manage cards
  • Receive banking notifications
Never share your banking password, PIN, one-time security code or authentication details with another person.

6. Salary payments

If you work in Croatia, your employer may pay your salary into your Croatian bank account according to your employment arrangements.

Check your employment contract and bank account details carefully.

7. Bank fees

Banks can charge fees for certain services. Fees depend on the bank, account type and service.

  • Account maintenance
  • Cash withdrawals
  • Transfers
  • Cards and additional services
  • Other banking services

Always check the current fee schedule of your bank.

8. If your card is lost or stolen

Contact your bank as soon as possible and ask them to block the card. If necessary, follow the bank's instructions for replacing it.

9. Useful banking habits

  • Keep your bank contact details saved.
  • Check transactions regularly.
  • Keep your PIN confidential.
  • Use official banking applications.
  • Be careful with suspicious messages and links.
  • Keep important banking documents safely.

10. Official information

For current banking information, always check the official website of your bank and relevant Croatian institutions.

Croatian National Bank (HNB) gov.hr

11. Important

Banking rules, fees, documents and requirements can change. Always confirm the latest information directly with your bank or the relevant official institution.

CROPATH provides general information for foreigners in Croatia. It is not legal, financial or banking advice.
