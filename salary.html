Salary & Work Rights

Important information about salary, employment rights, contracts and working conditions for people working in Croatia.

Last verified: 7 October 2026

💶 Salary Basics

Your employment contract should clearly state the salary and important employment conditions.

2026 minimum gross salary: €1,050 per month.

Gross salary is not the same as the amount you receive in your bank account. Taxes and contributions are handled according to Croatian rules.

Official source — Invest Croatia: Salaries →

👷 Employment Rights

Foreign workers and Croatian workers have employment rights under Croatian labour legislation.

Important: Workers should receive protection concerning employment conditions and occupational safety according to applicable Croatian law.

Working time and additional pay

Croatian rules provide increased pay for certain types of work, including overtime, night work, Sunday work and work on holidays, according to applicable rules.

Official source — Ministry of Labour →

Official source — Invest Croatia: Salaries →

🪪 Foreign Workers & Work Permits

Third-country nationals generally need the appropriate stay-and-work permit or another legally applicable basis before working in Croatia.

Very important: Having a residence permit does not automatically mean that you can work in any job or for any employer.

Where a stay-and-work permit applies, the worker may generally work only in the job and with the employer covered by the applicable permit.

Labour market testing and an HZZ opinion may also be required in relevant cases.

Official source — Croatian Ministry of the Interior (MUP) →

Official source — gov.hr: Work of Foreign Nationals →

📄 Before You Sign a Job Contract

Before starting work, check these important points:

  • Employer's full legal name
  • Your job position
  • Gross salary
  • Working hours
  • Overtime conditions
  • Holiday and Sunday work conditions
  • Start date of employment
  • Contract duration
  • Place of work
  • Whether your work permit covers the employer and job
Tip: Keep a copy of your signed employment contract and important salary documents.

⚠️ If Your Salary Is Not Paid

If an employer does not pay your salary or there is a serious problem with your employment conditions, keep copies of your contract, payslips, bank records and other relevant documents.

For immigration or work-permit issues, contact the competent Croatian authority before changing employer or starting another job.

Do not assume: A new employer can always be started immediately just because you already have a Croatian residence card.
