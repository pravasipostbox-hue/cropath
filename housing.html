🇭🇷 Housing Information Guide

Housing in Croatia

Practical information about renting a home, contracts, deposits, address registration and common housing issues in Croatia.

✓ Information checked: 7 October 2026

🏠 Finding a Home

Foreigners in Croatia can find accommodation through property websites, real-estate agencies, local advertisements and personal contacts.

Before paying money or signing an agreement, check the property, landlord and rental conditions carefully.

Tip: Always visit the property or verify the accommodation before making a significant payment.

🔑 Renting an Apartment

When renting a home, discuss the monthly rent, deposit, utilities, contract period and notice period before moving in.

Check these points

  • Monthly rent amount
  • Security deposit
  • Utility and service costs
  • Contract duration
  • Notice period
  • Furniture and equipment included

📄 Rental Contract

A written rental agreement is important because it records the conditions agreed between the landlord and tenant.

The contract should clearly state

  • Landlord and tenant information
  • Property address
  • Monthly rent
  • Deposit amount
  • Payment arrangements
  • Contract period
  • Termination or notice conditions
Important: Read the contract carefully before signing. Keep a copy of the signed agreement.

💶 Deposit and Payments

Landlords may request a security deposit. The amount and conditions should be agreed before payment.

  • Ask for a receipt or proof of payment.
  • Keep records of rent payments.
  • Clarify when the deposit will be returned.
  • Check whether unpaid bills can be deducted from the deposit.
Be careful: Do not transfer a large amount of money without verifying the property and the person receiving the payment.

💡 Utilities and Bills

Housing costs can include electricity, water, heating, gas, internet, building charges and other services.

Before moving in

  • Ask which bills are included in the rent.
  • Ask which bills must be paid separately.
  • Check the condition and meter readings when appropriate.
  • Keep copies of important bills and payments.

📍 Address Registration

Foreigners staying in Croatia may have obligations related to reporting or registering their address depending on their residence and stay status.

If you need to register your address for residence purposes, check the current requirements with the competent police administration or police station.

Important: Do not assume that signing a rental contract automatically completes any required residence registration.

🛋️ Moving Into a New Home

Before moving your belongings into the apartment, check the condition of the property.

  • Take photos of existing damage.
  • Check furniture and appliances.
  • Check electricity, water and heating.
  • Check keys and access cards.
  • Keep copies of important documents.

⚠️ Common Problems

  • Unexpected additional charges
  • Unclear deposit conditions
  • No written rental agreement
  • Problems with utilities
  • Disagreement about property damage
  • Unclear address-registration responsibilities
If there is a serious dispute: Keep documents, payment records, messages and photographs. Consider obtaining appropriate legal or professional advice.

🌍 Housing for Foreign Workers

If accommodation is provided by an employer, carefully check whether it is free, deducted from salary or subject to a separate agreement.

  • Ask where the accommodation is located.
  • Ask how many people share the accommodation.
  • Ask whether utilities are included.
  • Ask whether accommodation costs are deducted from salary.
  • Keep written information about the accommodation arrangement.
