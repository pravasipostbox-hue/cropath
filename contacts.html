When You Need Help

1. Residence or foreigners' documents
Contact MUP or the appropriate police administration/station.
2. Employment
Contact HZZ for employment-related information and services.
3. Health insurance
Contact HZZO for health-insurance information and rights.
4. Taxes
Use e-Porezna or contact the Croatian Tax Administration.
5. Emergency
In an actual emergency, use the appropriate Croatian emergency number. For general emergencies, call 112.
🌍 Language Help

If you have difficulty understanding an official procedure, ask the institution whether language assistance or an interpreter is available. Keep important documents and translations with you when visiting an office.
