Driving Licence in Croatia

Information about using foreign driving licences, exchanging licences, medical examinations, translations and applying through MUP.

Last verified: 7 October 2026

1. Foreign Driving Licence in Croatia

Foreign nationals may be able to drive in Croatia using a valid foreign driving licence, depending on their country of issue, residence status and applicable Croatian rules.

Always check the current requirements with MUP Croatia before applying for an exchange or other procedure.

2. EU / EEA Driving Licences

Driving licences issued by EU and EEA countries are generally recognised in Croatia, subject to the applicable EU rules and validity conditions.

3. Non-EU Driving Licences

If your driving licence was issued outside the EU/EEA, additional requirements may apply.

Check validity

Make sure your original licence is valid and readable.

Check residence

Your residence status in Croatia may affect the procedure.

Translation

A certified Croatian translation may be required.

Medical requirements

A medical examination may be required depending on the procedure.

4. Common Documents

  • Original driving licence
  • Passport or identity document
  • Proof of residence in Croatia
  • Photograph, if required
  • Medical certificate, if required
  • Certified Croatian translation, if required
  • Other documents requested by MUP

5. Medical Examination

Some driving licence procedures require a medical examination at an authorised medical institution.

Ask the medical provider which documents and identification you need before attending the examination.

6. Croatian Translation

If your licence or supporting documents are not accepted directly, a certified Croatian translation may be required.

Use an authorised translator and confirm the exact requirement before paying for a translation.

7. Applying Through MUP

  1. Check the applicable procedure.
  2. Prepare your original documents.
  3. Complete any required medical examination.
  4. Obtain a certified Croatian translation if required.
  5. Submit the application and documents to the competent MUP office.
Requirements can differ depending on the country that issued your licence and your individual situation. Confirm the current checklist with MUP before visiting.

8. Indian Driving Licence

Indian driving licence holders should check the current Croatian requirements before starting an exchange or recognition procedure.

  • Keep the original Indian driving licence.
  • Check whether additional verification is required.
  • Check translation requirements.
  • Prepare Croatian residence documents.
  • Confirm the current MUP procedure before applying.
Requirements can change. Always confirm the latest procedure with the competent Croatian authorities.

9. Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Do not rely only on old information from social media.
  • Do not submit photocopies when an original is required.
  • Do not assume every foreign licence has the same procedure.
  • Check translation requirements before ordering documents.
  • Confirm the correct MUP office before visiting.
