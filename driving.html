Driving Licence in Croatia

Practical information about foreign driving licences, Croatian licence exchange, documents, medical examination, translation and MUP procedures.

✓ Information checked: 7 October 2026

🚗 Foreign Driving Licence in Croatia

Foreign nationals living in Croatia may be able to drive using a valid foreign driving licence or may need to exchange it for a Croatian driving licence, depending on their country of issue, residence status and applicable rules.

Important: Do not assume that every foreign licence can be exchanged automatically. The requirements can depend on the country where the licence was issued and the applicable Croatian rules.

🇪🇺 EU / EEA Driving Licences

Driving licences issued by EU and EEA countries are subject to specific European and Croatian rules.

Before taking any action, check the current requirements with the competent Croatian authority.

🌍 Non-EU Driving Licences

If your driving licence was issued outside the EU/EEA, additional requirements may apply.

Depending on the country of issue and your circumstances, the process may involve documentation, translation, medical requirements or other verification.

Important: The exact exchange procedure should be confirmed with MUP before submitting an application.

📋 Common Documents

The exact documents depend on your situation. Commonly requested documents may include:

  • Valid passport or identification document
  • Valid foreign driving licence
  • Proof of residence or applicable residence document
  • Required medical certificate, where applicable
  • Certified translation, where required
  • Photograph or other identification material, where required
  • Additional documents requested by MUP
Tip: Do not prepare documents based only on an internet checklist. Confirm the current list with the competent MUP office.

🩺 Medical Examination

A medical examination or medical certificate may be required depending on the driving licence procedure and your individual circumstances.

Use an authorised medical provider and confirm the exact certificate required before attending the examination.

📝 Croatian Translation

Some foreign documents may need a Croatian translation.

The translation requirements can depend on the document and the authority handling your application.

Before paying for a translation: Confirm with MUP whether a certified Croatian translation is required for your specific licence and documents.

🏛️ Applying Through MUP

1

Check Eligibility

Confirm whether your foreign driving licence can be exchanged or whether another procedure applies.

2

Prepare Documents

Collect your identification, driving licence and any required supporting documents.

3

Medical / Translation

Complete any medical examination or translation requirements that apply to your case.

4

Submit the Application

Submit the required documents to the competent Croatian authority.

5

Follow the Procedure

Follow any additional instructions given by the competent authority.

Best practice: Always confirm the current requirements with MUP before starting the exchange process.

🇮🇳 Indian Driving Licence

If your driving licence was issued in India, additional verification or documentation may be required depending on the Croatian procedure applicable to your case.

Before visiting an authority

  • Keep your original Indian driving licence.
  • Check whether licence verification is required.
  • Check whether an official Croatian translation is required.
  • Check whether a medical certificate is required.
  • Confirm the current MUP document checklist.
Do not rely on an old checklist. Driving licence rules and document requirements can change. Confirm the current procedure before submitting documents.

❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using an expired driving licence.
  • Assuming every foreign licence can be exchanged.
  • Paying for unnecessary translations.
  • Going to the wrong MUP office.
  • Using outdated document lists.
  • Ignoring medical requirements where applicable.
