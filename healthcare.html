Healthcare in Croatia

Practical information about healthcare, HZZO, doctors, hospitals, pharmacies and emergency medical help.

✓ Information checked: 7 October 2026

🏥 Healthcare in Croatia

Croatia has a public healthcare system and healthcare services are provided through the Croatian Health Insurance Fund (HZZO) together with healthcare institutions and private providers.

Your access to healthcare can depend on your insurance status, residence status, employment and other circumstances.

Important: Always check your current HZZO status and the applicable healthcare rules for your individual situation.

💳 HZZO Health Insurance

HZZO is the Croatian Health Insurance Fund (Hrvatski zavod za zdravstveno osiguranje).

People who are covered by Croatian compulsory health insurance can use healthcare services according to the applicable rules and conditions.

Before using healthcare services

  • Check whether you have active health insurance.
  • Keep your health insurance information available.
  • Check which healthcare provider you can use.
  • Ask HZZO if you are unsure about your coverage.

👨‍⚕️ Family Doctor / General Practitioner

For many non-emergency health problems, the first point of contact can be a family doctor or general practitioner.

Your available healthcare provider can depend on your registration and insurance arrangements.

Tip: If you are registered with a family doctor, contact the doctor's office first for routine medical problems and appointments.

🏨 Hospitals

Hospitals provide specialist, diagnostic and emergency services depending on the hospital and department.

For planned treatment, check whether a referral, appointment or other documentation is required.

Do not delay emergency care. If the situation is serious or life-threatening, call the emergency medical service immediately.

💊 Pharmacies

Pharmacies in Croatia are called ljekarne.

Pharmacies can provide medicines and other health-related products according to Croatian rules.

  • Bring your prescription when required.
  • Ask the pharmacist if you need help understanding a medicine.
  • Check pharmacy opening hours before visiting.

🚨 Emergency Medical Help

112 — General emergency number

194 — Emergency medical service

If there is an immediate danger to life or serious injury, seek emergency medical help without delay.

📋 Before Visiting a Doctor

  • Take your identification document.
  • Keep your health insurance information available.
  • Take previous medical documents when relevant.
  • Bring a list of medicines you currently use.
  • Check whether an appointment is required.

🌍 Foreigners in Croatia

Healthcare rights and obligations can differ depending on your residence, employment, insurance status, European health insurance coverage and other circumstances.

Important: Do not assume that having a residence permit automatically means every healthcare service is free of charge.

Check your individual insurance status with HZZO or the competent healthcare institution.
