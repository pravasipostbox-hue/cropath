Healthcare in Croatia

Practical information about healthcare, HZZO, doctors, hospitals, pharmacies and emergency medical help.

✓ Information checked: 7 October 2026

🏥 Healthcare in Croatia

Croatia has a public healthcare system. Healthcare services are provided through HZZO, healthcare institutions and private providers.

Access to healthcare can depend on your insurance, residence status, employment and individual circumstances.

Important: Check your current HZZO status and the healthcare rules that apply to your situation.

💳 HZZO Health Insurance

HZZO is the Croatian Health Insurance Fund (Hrvatski zavod za zdravstveno osiguranje).

People covered by Croatian compulsory health insurance can use healthcare services according to applicable rules.

Before using healthcare services

  • Check whether your health insurance is active.
  • Keep your insurance information available.
  • Check which healthcare provider you can use.
  • Contact HZZO if you are unsure about your coverage.

👨‍⚕️ Family Doctor / General Practitioner

For many non-emergency health problems, your first contact can be a family doctor or general practitioner.

The healthcare provider available to you may depend on your registration and insurance arrangements.

Tip: For routine medical problems, contact your registered family doctor to ask about appointments.

🏨 Hospitals

Hospitals provide specialist, diagnostic and emergency services, depending on the hospital and department.

For planned treatment, check whether a referral, appointment or other documents are required.

Do not delay emergency care. If the situation is serious or life-threatening, seek emergency help immediately.

💊 Pharmacies

Pharmacies in Croatia are called ljekarne.

Pharmacies provide medicines and other health-related products according to Croatian rules.

  • Bring your prescription when required.
  • Ask the pharmacist if you need help understanding a medicine.
  • Check pharmacy opening hours before visiting.

🚨 Emergency Medical Help

112 — General emergency number

194 — Emergency medical service

If there is immediate danger to life or a serious injury, seek emergency medical help without delay.

📋 Before Visiting a Doctor

  • Take your identification document.
  • Keep your health insurance information available.
  • Take previous medical documents when relevant.
  • Bring a list of medicines you currently use.
  • Check whether an appointment is required.

🌍 Foreigners in Croatia

Healthcare rights and obligations can differ depending on residence, employment, insurance, European health insurance coverage and other circumstances.

Important: Do not assume that a residence permit automatically makes every healthcare service free.

Check your individual insurance status with HZZO or the relevant healthcare institution.
