Residence in Croatia

Practical information about residence permits and residence procedures for foreigners living in Croatia.

Last verified: 7 October 2026

🏠 Temporary Residence

Third-country nationals who want to stay in Croatia for longer than permitted by short-term stay rules may need to obtain temporary residence, depending on their circumstances and legal basis.

The application and supporting documents depend on the purpose of residence, such as work, family reunification, study or other permitted purposes.

📄 Common Residence Documents

The exact documents depend on the purpose of residence. Authorities may require documents proving the reason for stay and other conditions prescribed by Croatian law.

  • Valid passport or travel document
  • Proof of the purpose of residence
  • Proof of accommodation, where required
  • Proof of sufficient means of subsistence, where required
  • Health insurance, where required
  • Other documents depending on the residence category

⚠️ Important

Residence status and the right to work are related but are not always the same thing.

If you want to work in Croatia, check the specific work and residence conditions that apply to your situation before starting employment.

👮 Where to Apply

Residence matters for foreigners are handled by the competent Croatian police administration or police station according to the applicable procedure and place of residence.

Requirements and procedures can change. Always confirm the current requirements with the competent authority before submitting an application.

🔎 Before Applying

  • Check which residence category applies to you.
  • Check the current document requirements.
  • Check where your application must be submitted.
  • Check deadlines and validity dates.
  • Keep copies of documents submitted to the authorities.
  • Confirm the latest information with MUP.
