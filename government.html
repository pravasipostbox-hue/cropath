What Can You Do Online?

Check Government Information
Find official information about residence, work, taxes, healthcare and other public services.

Where available, use electronic public services instead of visiting an office.
Check Your Tax Information
Use the official Tax Administration services for applicable tax procedures and electronic documents.
Check Employment Services
Use HZZ information for employment-related services and labour-market information.

Before Using a Government Service

  • Keep your passport or identity document available.
  • Keep your Croatian residence document available when relevant.
  • Check whether an appointment is required.
  • Use the official government website for current requirements.
  • Check whether documents need Croatian translation.
  • Keep copies of important documents and confirmations.
⚠️ Important Government procedures and requirements can change. MALAYALIS IN CROATIA is an information guide and is not a Croatian government website. Always confirm the latest requirements with the competent Croatian authority before submitting documents or making important decisions.
