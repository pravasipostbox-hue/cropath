Work in Croatia

Practical information about working in Croatia for foreign nationals, based on official Croatian government sources.

Can foreigners work in Croatia?

Yes. Depending on their status and circumstances, foreign nationals may work in Croatia under the conditions established by Croatian law.

Third-country nationals generally need the appropriate residence and work authorization before starting employment in Croatia, unless an exemption applies.

Residence and work permit

A third-country national may generally work in Croatia on the basis of a residence and work permit or, where applicable, a work registration certificate.

The authorization can be connected to specific employment conditions, including the employer and job for which the authorization was issued.

⚠️ Important

Do not assume that having a Croatian residence permit automatically gives you the right to work.

Before starting a job, check whether your particular status allows you to work and whether a residence and work permit or another authorization is required.

Labour market test

For certain employment situations, the Croatian Employment Service (HZZ) may be involved in the procedure before a residence and work permit is issued.

The exact procedure depends on the type of employment and the applicable legal rules.

Before you start a job

  • Check your residence status.
  • Check whether you have the right to work.
  • Check whether a residence and work permit is required.
  • Check the employer and job details connected with your authorization.
  • Use official Croatian sources for the latest requirements.
